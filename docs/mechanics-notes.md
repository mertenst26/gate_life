# Gate Life -- Mechanics Reference (from Rifts Ultimate Edition)

## Combat (Melee Round = 15 seconds)
- Initiative: d20 + bonuses, re-rolled each round, ties re-rolled between tied combatants
- Attacks per Melee (APM): from Hand-to-Hand skill table + class bonuses
- Strike: d20 + bonuses. 4 or less = miss. 5+ = hit. Natural 20 = critical (double damage, only blocked by nat 20). Natural 1 = always miss.
- Defense: defender rolls d20 + parry/dodge. Must meet or exceed attacker's strike. Ties go to defender.
  - Parry: free with H2H training (no action cost)
  - Dodge: costs 1 action unless character has auto-dodge
- Damage: weapon dice + PS bonuses (physical). MD weapons hit MDC armor first; when armor depleted, hits character directly. SDC weapons cannot damage MDC armor/creatures.
- Roll with Impact: costs 1 action after physical hit. d20 + bonuses >= attacker's strike = half damage.
- Excess attacks: if combatant has more APM than opponent, remaining opponent can only parry (free) not dodge. Dodge borrows from next round.

## Damage System (SDC/MDC Duality)
- SDC (Structural Damage Capacity): normal damage scale (punches, knives, normal guns)
- MDC (Mega-Damage Capacity): mega-damage scale (energy weapons, supernatural creatures, power armor)
- 1 MD = 100 SDC
- Characters have HP + SDC (body). Armor has MDC.
- MD weapons destroy armor MDC first, then overflow kills character
- SDC weapons cannot damage MDC armor or creatures

## Attributes (8 stats, 3d6 each, 16-18 gets bonus d6)
- IQ: Intelligence
- ME: Mental Endurance (psionic saves, ISP base)
- MA: Mental Affinity (social skills, trust/intimidate)
- PS: Physical Strength (melee damage bonus)
- PP: Physical Prowess (strike/parry/dodge bonuses)
- PE: Physical Endurance (HP base, save vs poison/magic)
- PB: Physical Beauty (social modifier)
- SPD: Speed (movement rate)

## Psionics
- ISP (Inner Strength Points): power pool for psionic abilities
- Recovery: 2 ISP/hour during rest, 6 ISP/hour during meditation
- Save vs Psionics: 12+ on d20 (ME bonuses apply)
- Sensitive powers: perception/detection category (Dog Boy specialty)

## XP Awards (Rifts Subjective Method)
- Playing in character: 50 XP
- Clever idea (successful): 100 XP
- Quick thinking: 50-100 XP
- Heroic action: 50-100 XP
- Defeating minor threat: 25-50 XP
- Defeating major threat: 75-100 XP
- Defeating great menace: 150-400 XP
- Critical plan saving group: 400-1000 XP
- Self-sacrifice: 500-700 XP
- Skill use: 25 XP

## Dog Boy RCC Specifics
- Base HP = PE attribute value
- SDC: 40
- ISP: ME + 10, recovers per psionic rules
- PPE: 18 (6d6 average)
- APM: 4 at level 1
- Combat bonuses: +2 initiative, +2 parry, +2 dodge
- Innate sensing: psychic/magic energy (50ft), active psionics (500ft), supernatural beings (1000ft)
- Psionic category: Sensitive
- Max level: 15
